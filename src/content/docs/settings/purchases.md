The purchase sections sit at the bottom of the main **Settings** screen.

:::tip[Nothing here is required to run a game]
Encounter+ runs a complete game out of the box. The library and compendium, creating and editing
creatures, items and spells, encounter building, the initiative tracker, campaigns and modules,
importing and exporting content, roll tables and the dice roller are all there without any purchase
at all.

The **Premium subscription in particular is entirely optional.** It is not a licence for the app and
it does not gate running a full-fledged game — it adds extras on top, and its main purpose is to
support continued development and regular updates. Plenty of people run whole campaigns without one.
:::

## One-time Purchases

### Battle Map

A single, permanent purchase that unlocks the battle map: maps, tokens, walls and lighting, line of
sight and fog of war, drawing tools, markers, and the
[Battle Map settings screen](/settings/battle-map/).

Buy it once and it is yours — no renewal, nothing to keep paying. It is the only one-time purchase
currently offered, and the row shows *Purchased* once you own it.

### Restore Purchases

Re-checks the App Store for everything your Apple Account has already bought and restores it.

Use it after reinstalling the app, on a new device, or if a purchase you own is not being recognised.
It costs nothing and never charges you again. Purchases from older versions of the app are honoured
here too.

## Ongoing Purchases

### Premium Subscription

Optional, and aimed at supporting further development. The row shows your current status; tapping it
opens the subscription screen, which offers:

| Option | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Monthly** | Billed each month. |
| **Yearly** | Billed once a year. |

Both start with a **7-day free trial**. When the trial ends the subscription begins and renews
automatically; you can cancel any time from your Apple Account settings. Cancel at least 24 hours
before a period ends to avoid being charged for the next one.

#### What's included

- Early access to upcoming features
- 3D dice with multiple themes and styles — see [Dice Roller](/settings/dice-roller/#type)
- Web server and remote play (beta) — see [Remote Play](/settings/remote-play/)
- Animated tiles, auras and areas of effect
- Weather effects (rain, snow, fog)
- Additional party movement modes
- Everything included in the Battle Map purchase

That last point is worth noting: a subscription already covers the battle map, so there is no need to
buy both. If you subscribe first and later want to keep the map permanently, buy the Battle Map
purchase — it survives the subscription lapsing, where the premium-only features do not.

A subscription is the only purchase that can end. If it lapses, the premium-only features become
unavailable again; nothing you created is lost, and anything you own outright stays yours.

## Additional Support

### Tip Jar

Four one-off tips — **Small**, **Medium**, **Large** and **Huge** — for anyone who wants to support
the app beyond what they have already paid.

Tips unlock nothing. They are purely a thank-you, and they can be given as often as you like.

## Notes

- Prices are shown in your local currency and are set by the App Store. A row showing no price yet
  is still loading; *Not available* means the store could not be reached or purchases are restricted
  on the device.
- Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to the device — use **Restore Purchases** on your
  other devices.
- Refunds are handled by Apple, not by Encounter+. Use
  [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
